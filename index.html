Scooby

A plain medication tracker — for pets and for people.

Read this first

This app is a notebook, not a medical device. It records what you tell it and counts the hours. That's all it does.

  • It is not medical or veterinary advice, and no substitute for a vet, doctor, pharmacist, or nurse.
  • It does not check doses, interactions, allergies, or whether a medication is right for anyone. Every dose and time you enter comes from you.
  • Always follow the label and the instructions you were given. If this app and the label disagree, the label is right.
  • Reminders can fail — a phone dies, an alarm is missed, a page is closed. Don't rely on it alone for anything that matters.
  • The suggested schedules in the medication list are common starting points for filling in the form, not a prescription.
  • In an emergency, call your vet, your doctor, or emergency services. In the US: Poison Control 1-800-222-1222, Pet Poison Helpline 1-855-764-7661, ASPCA Poison Control 1-888-426-4435.
  • Provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. You are responsible for what you give and when.

Everything you enter stays on this device.
